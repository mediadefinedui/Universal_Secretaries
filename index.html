FAQ

Before you book

Everything you need to know about company registration, tax advisory, and consultation bookings in Sri Lanka.

7 questions & answers
01

How do I register a company in Sri Lanka?

Company registration in Sri Lanka is done online through the Registrar of Companies eROC system. You reserve a name, file Forms 1, 18 and 19, and since 30 March 2026 file BO 5 and BO 1 on the beneficial ownership portal. A Company Secretary must be appointed under Section 221. We handle each step for you, from the first consultation to the certificate.

02

Do you offer tax consultation for companies and individuals?

Yes. Tax consultation covers individual and company tax planning, VAT compliance, capital gains tax (CGT), income tax returns, self-assessment payments, IRD correspondence, and EPF and ETF.

03

What does your online bookkeeping service include?

Our online bookkeeping service for Sri Lankan companies covers ledgers, bank and balance sheet reconciliations, payables and receivables, and records ready for your auditor and the IRD, on a fixed monthly scope agreed upfront.

04

What is beneficial ownership filing?

Since 30 March 2026, companies must declare the people who ultimately own or control them on the Registrar's BO portal. New companies file BO 5 and BO 1 at incorporation. Existing companies file BO 7, and changes after share issues or transfers go on BO 2 or BO 3. Penalties for not complying are serious, and directors can be held personally liable.

05

What do I need for the registration consultation?

Proposed company names, the directors and shareholders, the beneficial owners and the business activity. If you do not have these yet, book anyway and we will tell you what to prepare.

06

Does every company need a Company Secretary?

Yes. Section 221 of the Companies Act No. 07 of 2007 requires one. Our team includes a qualified Company Secretary who can act in that role.

07

Can I book from outside Sri Lanka?

Yes. Consultations run by video or phone, and the booking panel shows Sri Lanka time with your own time zone beside it.
