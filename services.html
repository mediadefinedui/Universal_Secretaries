COMPANY REGISTRATION IN SRI LANKA

We register your Pvt Ltd company on eROC, file the beneficial ownership forms the Registrar now requires, and stay on as your Company Secretary.

  • Name approval and Section 7 checks on eROC
  • Preparation & filing of Forms 1, 18, and 19 on eROC
  • Beneficial ownership: BO 5 and BO 1 at incorporation
  • BO 2, BO 3, BO 4 and BO 7 for existing companies
  • Qualified Company Secretary appointment under Section 221
  • Annual Return (Form 15) and statutory filings on Forms 13, 20 and 6
